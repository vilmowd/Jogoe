Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy explains how Jogoe (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit this website or use the Jogoe Windows app. Jogoe is built to keep most of your data on your computer.

Who we are

Jogoe is a desktop AI companion. Contact us at hello@jogoe.app.

This website

When you check out, we ask for your name and email so we can attach a license in this browser. That license is stored in your browser’s local storage. We do not create an account on a Jogoe server as part of this checkout.

If we enable a Stripe Payment Link, Stripe processes the payment. Stripe’s own privacy policy then applies to card details. We never see your full card number.

This site may be hosted by GitHub Pages or a similar host. The host may collect standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested.

The Jogoe app

Chat history, remembered facts, and settings stay on your PC (typically under your Windows app data folder). Jogoe does not upload your conversations to a Jogoe cloud for training or advertising.

If you ask Jogoe to open a document or search files, it reads those files on your machine to complete the request. If you ask it to remember something, it stores that fact locally until you edit or delete it in Settings.

If you ask for live information (weather, news, a web lookup), Jogoe sends the search query to third-party public sources such as DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia, or a weather service. You can turn this off in Settings → Memory.

Voice features:

  • Listening uses your microphone only when you start a voice request.
  • If the optional speech library is installed, audio may be sent to Google’s speech service to turn speech into text.
  • Otherwise Jogoe uses Windows speech recognition on your PC.
  • Natural voices (Jenny, Aria, Guy, and similar) use Microsoft neural speech and need the internet. Classic Windows voices such as Zira and David stay offline.

The first install may download Ollama and a local language model to your PC. After that, chat with the model can run without sending the conversation to us.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your chats to train a public model.
  • We do not require an account to download after checkout in this browser.

How long data stays

Website license data stays in your browser until you clear site data. App memory and settings stay on your PC until you delete them or uninstall Jogoe. Host logs follow the host’s retention rules.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete remembered facts in Settings → Memory.
  • Turn off live internet lookups in Settings.
  • Use offline Windows voices instead of neural voices.
  • Clear this site’s data in your browser to remove the local license record.
  • Email hello@jogoe.app if you want help deleting information we hold, or if you have a privacy question.

Children

Jogoe is not directed at children under 13. Do not use checkout or the app if you are under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page.
